# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 19, 2026_

Stereo Camera (`io.github.vladimir_kalinin.stereocamera`) is a camera app developed by
Vladimir Kalinin. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what it does with it.

## Summary

The app does not collect, share or sell any personal data. Photos, videos and sound never leave
the device. The only data sent off the device are crash reports, and they can be turned off in Settings.
The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

## Crash reports

When the app crashes or a camera fails to start, a crash report is sent to the developer through
[Firebase Crashlytics](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) (Google). A report contains:

- the error and the state of the app's code at that moment (stack trace, recent app log messages);
- the phone model, Android version, free memory and disk space, and whether the app was in the foreground;
- the camera setup the app chose (camera ids, stream sizes, capture mode) and the phone's temperature status;
- a random installation identifier created by Crashlytics, so that several crashes on one phone can be told apart.

Reports contain no photos, videos, sound, location, contacts or account data. They are used only to find
and fix bugs. Firebase keeps crash reports for 90 days. You can turn crash reports off in
Settings → "Send crash reports".

## Permissions

- **Camera** — to show the preview and take stereo photos and videos.
- **Microphone** — to record sound for videos. Audio is recorded only while video recording is in progress.

Photos and videos are saved only on your device, in the `DCIM/3D Camera` album.
They never leave the device unless you share them yourself.

## Data stored on the device

The app keeps its settings (camera alignment, preview mode, video format) in the app's private storage.
Android may include these settings in your device backup according to your system backup settings.
Uninstalling the app removes them.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.
